Problem and challenge
  • Tattoos are generally irreversible, the cost of removal is pretty high, and it is not possible to be fully restored, so communicating with clients is extremely crucial.
  • Even though there are many tattoo-related applications in app stores, however, the biggest problem is that most of them only represent details with 2D photos.
  • The increasing cost of communication between tattoo artists and customers is leading to a lack of trust between them.

3D workspace and controllable tattoo image projectors
This approach is convenient for tattoo artists to efficiently communicate with customers.

Tattoo artists can rotate the 3d model 360 degrees, and add multiple tattoo image projectors in the workspace, they can can also be move, scale, and rotate these projectors.


Replace the existing projected tattoo image by uploading an new image
Tattoo artist can replace the existing image by uploading any new image if they need.

For customers, they can try to talk with artists to choose tattoo images they like.


Hide projectors to preview the result
Tattoo artists can choose to hide all tattoo image projectors to show customers the result.

The customers can concisely understand the final result and decide if this is what they want.

Agreement e-signature
After several communication, if both of tattoo artists and customers reach consensus, then a written agreement is a must.

Tattoo artists can update agreement terms and have the names of both party signed with full communication with customers.
