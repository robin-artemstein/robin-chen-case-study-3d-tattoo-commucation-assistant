Overview
The project is developed for an tattoo store, and for tattoo artists to commnuicate with customers, and reduce the communication cost for tattoo artists and customers.
Outcome and impact
  • Customers satisfication rates increase by 75%.
  • Tattoo artists reduce communication costs by 60%.
  • Customer complaint rates and consumer disputes decreased by 97%.
Problem and challenge
  • Tattoos are generally irreversible, the cost of removal is pretty high, and it is not possible to be fully restored, so communicating with clients is extremely crucial.
  • Even though there are many tattoo-related applications in app stores, however, the biggest problem is that most of them only represent details with 2D photos.
  • The increasing cost of communication between tattoo artists and customers is leading to a lack of trust between them.
