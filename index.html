Problem and challenge
  • Tattoos are generally irreversible, the cost of removal is pretty high, and it is not possible to be fully restored, so communicating with clients is extremely crucial.
  • Even though there are many tattoo-related applications in app stores, however, the biggest problem is that most of them only represent details with 2D photos.
  • The increasing cost of communication between tatoo artists and customers is leading to a lack of trust between them.
My approach
  • Unified Android tablet PC hardware and an application
  • Fully interactive 3D workspace
  • Agreement management
Unified Android tablet PC hardwares and an application
According to client's requirements, my first propose is unified hardware and an application for tattoo artists.
After couple times of comparasion, I and tattoo artists had unanimously decide to pick Samsung Galsxy Tab, which come with a dedicated EMR digitizer, because tattoo artists need precise control in the application.
Fully interactive 3D workspace
With this design, tattoo artists can rotate the 3d model 360 degrees, and add multiple tattoo image projectors in the workspace, they can also move, scale, rotate, and set the visibility of these projectors. Tattoo artists can replace the defalt tattoo image by uploading what they need from local machine,
Tattoo artists can show the screen to customers while they are working on it, customers can preview the result and tell artists if this is what they want.
Agreement management
After several communication, if both of tattoo artists and customers reach consensus, then a written agreement is a must.
Tattoo artists can update agreement terms and have the names of both party signed with full communication with customers, then tattoo artists can save the signature on their cloud and print them on the paper for customers to reserve.
